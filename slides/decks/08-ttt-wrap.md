# Demo vs drive

Demo when:

- The step is risky or subscription-sensitive
- The room needs a shared pattern
- The tool is unfamiliar

Let learners drive when:

- Evidence must be owned by the team
- Copilot output needs review
- There are multiple valid design choices

<!-- Speaker note: The best MicroHack energy comes from learners driving evidence, not watching a full-day demo. -->

# Prep checklist pointer

Before running a cohort:

- Confirm subscriptions, quota and region
- Validate dev container or Codespaces path
- Test Copilot skills and MCP servers
- Review facilitator guides and solutions
- Prepare fallback manual paths
- Set expectations for evidence collection

<!-- Speaker note: Point trainers to the prep checklist in the trainer materials. Do not turn the wrap into a setup lab. -->

---

# Common delivery pitfalls

- Starting without preflight completion
- Letting Copilot replace human review
- Losing time in deep debugging
- Under-scoping evidence artifacts
- Skipping sign-off or operations evidence
- Forgetting the mixed-cohort learning model

<!-- Speaker note: Share your own delivery risks if relevant. Trainers learn from concrete failure modes. -->

---

# Adapt for your cohort

- Keep controls and evidence fixed
- Adjust examples and pairings
- Add primers for newcomers
- Add stretch goals for experts
- Keep lab outputs small enough to review
- Debrief after every module

<!-- Speaker note: Adaptation is allowed, but the specialization evidence must remain intact. -->
